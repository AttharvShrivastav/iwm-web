# IWM Content Preparation Checklist & Guidelines

Use this guide to prepare content for the complete IWM website. Following these guidelines ensures the design remains balanced, readable, and premium.

---

## 1. Global Components (All Pages)

### Page Hero Sections
*Shared by About, Services, & People pages.*
- **Section Label**: `+ LABEL` (e.g., `+ OUR SERVICES`). **Max 2-3 words.**
- **Title**: High-impact primary headline. **Max 10-12 words.**
- **Subtitle**: Supporting context below the headline. **Max 35-40 words.**
- **CTA Label**: Text for the navigation link (e.g., `READ OUR STORY`). **Max 3 words.**

---

## 2. Home Page Specifics

### Main Hero (`Hero.tsx`)
- **Main Heading**: The large text that appears in the center. **Max 8-10 words.**
- **Bottom Content Label**: Supporting tagline (e.g., `RADICAL DIGNITY`). **Max 2 words.**
- **Bottom Content Description**: Brief brand summary. **Max 20 words.**

### Impact Section (`Impact.tsx`)
- **Cards (x3)**:
    - **Statistic**: The large number (e.g., `100+`). **Max 5 characters.**
    - **Label**: What the stat represents (e.g., `Vehicles in Operation`). **Max 3 words.**
    - **Description**: Supporting detail below the stat. **Max 15 words.**

### Mission & Cleanliness Statement (`Cleanliness.tsx`)
- **Staggered Reveal Lines (x4)**: Large impact text that fades in on scroll. **Max 8 words per line.**
- **Mission Paragraph**: Supporting body text at the bottom. **Max 40 words.**

### Discover Services Spotlight (`DiscoverServices.tsx`)
- **Service Name**: Headlines for the horizontal spotlight carousel. **Max 5 words.**
- **Metadata**: Short context (e.g., `Operations`). **Max 2 words.**

### Team Intro Statement (`MeetTheTeam.tsx`)
- **Reveal Heading**: Large text that highlights as you scroll. **Max 25-30 words.**
- **Follow-up CTA**: Button text (Default: `PEOPLE`). **Max 2 words.**

### Industries Section (`Industries.tsx`)
- **Industry Name**: Sector headline (e.g., `SMART CITIES`). **Max 3 words.**
- **Short Summary**: Brief description. **Max 20 words.**

### Clients/Partner Marquee (`ClientsSection.tsx`)
- **Section Heading**: Title above the marquee. **Max 6-8 words.**
- **Logos**: High-quality PNGs with transparent backgrounds.

---

## 3. About Page Specifics

### Our Story (`OurStory.tsx`)
- **Vertical Accent Title**: Short label on the left. **Max 4 words.**
- **Body Paragraphs (x2-3)**: Detailed history/vision. **Max 60 words per paragraph.**

### About Values Pillars (`AboutValues.tsx`)
- **Pillar Heading**: The core value title. **Max 2 words.**
- **Description**: The commitment behind it. **Max 40 words.**

### Founder's Note (`FoundersNote.tsx`)
- **Heading**: catchphrase/opener. **Max 10 words.**
- **Body Content**: The personal message. **Max 150 words total** (split into 3 paragraphs).
- **Founder Details**: Name and Designation. **Max 5 words each.**

---

## 4. Services Page Specifics

### Services Brand Statement (`ValuesSection.tsx`)
- **Main Headline**: Left-side large text. **Max 15 words.**
- **Detail List (x3)**: Supporting pillars on the right. **Max 30 words each.**

### Service List Grid (`ServiceList.tsx`)
- **Service Title**: **Max 4 words.**
- **Brief Summary**: **Max 25 words.**

### Machinery Carousel (`MachinerySection.tsx`)
- **Machine Name**: **Max 5 words.**
- **Category**: Short tag (e.g., `WASTE LOGISTICS`). **Max 3 words.**
- **Description**: Technical summary. **Max 30 words.**
- **Specs (x3 per machine)**:
    - **Label**: (e.g., `CAPACITY`). **Max 2 words.**
    - **Value**: (e.g., `150k m²/d`). **Max 3 words.**

---

## 5. People Page Specifics

### Leadership Team (`LeadershipSection.tsx`)
- **Name**: **Max 3 words.**
- **Designation**: **Max 4 words.**

### Sipahi Voices (Worker Testimonials) (`SipahiVoices.tsx`)
- **Worker Name & Role**: **Max 4 words each.**
- **Tenure**: (e.g., `5 Years`). **Max 2 words.**
- **Testimonial Statement**: Impactful story. **Max 60 words.** (Crucial to fit the single-viewport design).

### Careers Section (`CareersSection.tsx`)
- **Job Title**: **Max 5 words.**
- **Location & Type**: (e.g., `GWALIOR | FULL-TIME`). **Max 4 words.**
- **Short Summary**: Hook for the role. **Max 25 words.**

---

## 6. Job Applications (`ApplyPage.tsx`)

### Position Details
- **Role Summary**: Overview in the sidebar. **Max 35 words.**
- **Key Responsibilities (x5)**: Bulleted list. **Max 15 words per bullet.**
